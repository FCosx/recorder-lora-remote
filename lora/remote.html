About radio updates and recording

Topic lists load in small chunks over LoRa and may take time. Selected topics uses the fetched list; All available topics discovers the list at Start. New topics appearing later require a new recording. Status requests wait 2 seconds after each reply, plus radio transmission time. The recording timer advances locally between replies and is an estimate; other values show the latest Pi snapshot. Stop requests normal bag finalization on the Pi.
